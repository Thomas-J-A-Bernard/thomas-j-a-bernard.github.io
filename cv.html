Profile

Early-career geomorphologist interested in interactions among surface processes, tectonics, and climate, and in their influence on the large-scale evolution of mountain belts and foreland-basin systems. My research combines topographic analysis, thermo- and geochronology, numerical landscape-evolution models, and inverse modelling.
