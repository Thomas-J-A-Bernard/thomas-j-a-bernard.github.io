Exhumation and Landscape Evolution of the East-Central Greenland Margin

2014 - 2015

Reconstructing the long-term exhumation history of the East-Central Greenland margin from low-temperature thermochronology: Implication for glaciation, erosion and landscape evolution.

Post-Orogenic Evolution of the Mountain Range-Foreland Basin System of the Pyrenees

2016 - 2019

The project investigates how mountain belts and foreland basins evolve from late syn- to post-orogenesis. Using the Pyrenees, it explores the links between exhumation, landscape evolution, and sediment transfer long after tectonic activity has ceased.

Denudation Rate across Germany - Integrating Surface Analysis, Thermochronology, Cosmogenic Radionuclide and Landscape Evolution Modeling

2021 - 2023

Ensuring the long-term safety of nuclear waste repositories requires understanding how landscapes evolve over millions of years. This project reconstructs erosion histories and predicts future landscape evolution across Germany using geological observations and numerical modelling.

Quantifying Tectonics-Climate-Surface Process Interactions in the Qinling-Dabie Orogen Using Numerical Modeling

2026 - 2027

The Qinling–Dabie Mountains form the major topographic divide of Central-East China. This project reconstructs their long-term evolution to understand how mountain decay has influenced regional climate and landscape development.
