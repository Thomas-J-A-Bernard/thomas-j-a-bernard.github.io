About Me:

I am an early career associate professor at CUPB (China University of Petroleum, Beijing). My main research interests are about the interactions between the Earth's topography, tectonics and surface processes related to climate in mountaneous landscapes. I used mainly topographic data analyses, low-temperature thermochronology and landscape evolution modelling to study these topics. My study areas have included the central-east Greenland margin, Pyrenees, south-central Germany, and more recently mountain ranges in China such as Dabie Shan and Tian Shan. Please see my research page, for more detailled about my past and current research projects.
